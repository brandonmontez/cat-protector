# Code of Conduct

Cat Protector is a small project maintained by volunteers who would rather be watching a show with a cat on their lap. Keep it pleasant.

- Be kind and assume good intent. People arrive with different experience levels, keyboards, and cats.
- Critique code, not people. "This can leak the tap" is helpful; "this is dumb" is not.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind, in issues, pull requests, or anywhere else connected to the project.
- Respect maintainers' time. A clear bug report with your macOS version and steps to reproduce is the most generous thing you can give.

If someone is making the project unpleasant, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (their profile, or a private vulnerability report if the matter is sensitive). Reports are handled discreetly, and anyone who cannot follow these guidelines will be asked to leave.
